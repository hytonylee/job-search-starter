# Research: Northwind Health

Researched 2026-03-12.

## What they do

Booking and intake software for specialist clinics (physiotherapy, dermatology, imaging). Clinics pay per practitioner per month. Patients book through a web page or the clinic's own app.

## Size and stage

About 140 employees. Series B, raised in 2024. Offices in Toronto and Halifax. Hybrid, two days in the office.

## Recent news

- Launched a patient mobile app in January 2026.
- Hired a new VP Product in late 2025, previously at a logistics company.
- Layoffs: not found.

## How they work

Squads of six engineers with one designer. Their product blog describes quarterly planning and a weekly customer call that every PM joins. My read: discovery is expected, not optional.

## Interview process

Reported by two candidates on a review site: recruiter screen, hiring manager, a product case with a panel, then values. Reported, not confirmed.

## Why me, why them

- Their core metric is no-shows. My booking redesign moved exactly that number.
- They just launched a mobile app and I have web only. I should say so before they ask.

## Questions to ask them

1. The mobile app launched in January. What has it changed in how patients reschedule?
2. How does the scheduling squad split discovery between clinic staff and patients?
3. What did the new VP Product change in how the roadmap is set?

## Red flags and unknowns

- Revenue and churn: not found.
- Why the previous scheduling PM left: not found.
