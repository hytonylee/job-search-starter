# Screening Rubric

## Hard stops (any one means skip)

- [x] Salary range tops out below my floor: $125,000 base
- [x] On-site more than two days a week, or outside Toronto without remote
- [x] Requires people management experience (I do not have it)
- [x] Product Manager: the role is delivery or project management with no discovery
- [x] Growth Product Manager: no experimentation tooling and no analyst support

## Fit score

| Required items I meet with evidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| 70% or more | Apply today |
| 50 to 69% | Apply if the missing items are already in my gap list |
| Under 50% | Skip, and log the missing items anyway |

## Signals to be careful with

- "Product manager" reporting into engineering with no designer named
- Growth roles measured only on top-of-funnel signups
- No salary range and no named team
