# Mode: practice

## Exercises

### Product Manager: product case
1. Give me one case in two or three sentences: improve a product, a metric dropped, prioritise five requests, or should we build this.
2. I work through it aloud. You answer clarifying questions briefly and otherwise stay quiet.
3. Push back once on my weakest assumption.

**Score on:** Did I clarify the goal first? Did I pick a user and a problem? Did I name a metric? Did I make a decision?

**A rep for this role:** a one-page written teardown of a real product, with a recommendation.

### Growth Product Manager: experiment design
1. Give me a funnel with numbers at each step and one goal.
2. I choose where to intervene, state a hypothesis, and design the test: who sees it, the metric, the sample size, when it stops.
3. Ask what I would do if the result is flat.

**Score on:** Did I pick the step with the most leverage? Was the hypothesis falsifiable? Did I size the test? Did I name a guardrail metric?

**A rep for this role:** an experiment plan written from scratch for a public product, sized with a real calculator.
