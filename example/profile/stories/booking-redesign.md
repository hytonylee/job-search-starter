# The booking redesign

- **Competencies:** achievement, ambiguity, stakeholder
- **Roles:** all
- **When and where:** 2024, Brightlane, Product Manager
- **Last practised:** 2026-03-16
- **Used in:** Northwind Health, 2026-03-10 screen. Landed well, interviewer asked how I measured it

## Situation

Clinics were churning and blamed "scheduling". Nobody could say what that meant. No-shows were 18%.

## Task

I was asked to "fix booking" in one quarter with one squad. I had to decide what the problem actually was.

## Action

I interviewed 38 clinic administrators in three weeks. I found that patients could not reschedule without phoning, so they simply did not show up. I wrote a one-page spec for self-service rescheduling and cut two requested features to make room. I set the success metric before we built anything and built the dashboard for it myself.

## Result

No-show rate fell from 18% to 11% across 210 clinics, measured over the 90 days after launch on our own booking data. Two of the three clinics that had given notice stayed. I would interview patients as well next time; I only spoke to staff.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you know the redesign caused it?: Clinics that had not yet been migrated stayed at 18% over the same period.
- What did you cut?: Group bookings and a calendar sync. Sales was unhappy about the second one.
