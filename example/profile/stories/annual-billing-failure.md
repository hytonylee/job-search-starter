# The annual-billing experiment that failed

- **Competencies:** failure, learning, feedback
- **Roles:** Growth Product Manager
- **When and where:** 2025, Brightlane, Product Manager
- **Last practised:** 2026-03-11
- **Used in:** [not used yet]

## Situation

We wanted more cash up front, so I proposed defaulting the pricing page to annual billing.

## Task

It was my experiment: my hypothesis, my plan, my call on when to stop.

## Action

I launched it to half of new signups without first sizing how long it needed to run. By day 9 conversion in the test group was clearly lower. I stopped it, and I told the leadership team myself rather than leaving it in a report. My manager told me plainly that I had skipped the sizing step because I was sure I was right. I wrote a one-page checklist for sizing a test and used it for every experiment afterwards.

## Result

Paid conversion in the test group was 3.1% against 4.4% in control over 9 days, about 40 lost signups. The next 6 experiments all had a planned sample size and stopping rule before launch.

## Likely follow-ups

- Why did you not size it?: I was confident and in a hurry. That is the honest answer.
- What is on the checklist?: Baseline rate, smallest change worth detecting, sample size, the date we stop either way.
