# Cutting the roadmap in half

- **Competencies:** prioritisation, influence, conflict
- **Roles:** Product Manager
- **When and where:** 2025, Brightlane, Product Manager
- **Last practised:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Used in:** [not used yet]

## Situation

The 2025 roadmap had 14 items for two squads. We had finished 5 of 12 the year before.

## Task

I owned the roadmap and had to get it to something we could finish.

## Action

We scored every item on customer evidence and effort. We agreed on six. The head of sales disagreed strongly about two of the items we dropped, and we met three times before we settled it by promising a review at mid-year.

## Result

We finished all six. Sales accepted the plan.

## Likely follow-ups

- What did sales lose?: A custom reporting export for two large accounts.
