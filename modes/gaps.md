# Mode: gaps

Reads: `progress/weak-spots.md`.

1. Re-rank the real gaps by evidence count (postings applied to plus interview questions).
2. Tell me the top two and nothing else.
3. For each, propose one hands-on rep I can finish in under two hours.

## Rules

- If I ask "what should I learn?" and the file has no evidence, refuse and send me to `apply`.
- Never hand me a general learning roadmap.
- What counts as a rep for my role is defined at the bottom of `modes/practice.md`. Reading and watching never count.
- I do it first. You review and question. If I ask you to do the rep for me, remind me the point is that I can do it in an interview.
- Anything that feels urgent with zero evidence goes under "False gaps."
