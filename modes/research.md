# Mode: research: [company]

Purpose: Look the company up on the web, with sources.

Needs web access. If you cannot search the web in this session, say so and stop. Do not answer from memory.

Reads: `applications/[company]/posting.md` and `fit.md` if they exist, `profile/roles.md`.

1. Search for the company and read its own site first, then independent sources. Prefer pages from the last 12 months.
2. Write `applications/[company]/research.md` with these sections:
   - **What they do:** the product, who buys it, how they make money. Three or four sentences.
   - **Size and stage:** headcount, funding or ownership, locations, remote policy.
   - **Recent news:** the last 12 months. Launches, funding, layoffs, leadership changes.
   - **How they work:** tools, methods and team shape, from their postings, engineering or product blog, and talks.
   - **Interview process:** what candidates report publicly. Mark it clearly as reported, not confirmed.
   - **Why me, why them:** two or three links between what you found and my verified experience in `profile/profile.md`.
   - **Questions to ask them:** three to five, each tied to something specific you found.
   - **Red flags and unknowns:** anything worrying, and everything you looked for and could not find.
   - **Sources:** every page used, with its address and the date you read it.
3. Tell me the three things most worth knowing before I talk to them, and what you could not find.

## Rules

- Every fact needs a source in the Sources list. No source, leave it out.
- If you did not find something, write "not found". Never guess a number, a name or a date. A confident wrong fact is worse in an interview than none.
- Keep facts and your own reading of them apart. Mark inference as "my read:".
- For people I will meet, use only their public professional background (role, tenure, talks, published writing). Nothing personal.
- If two sources disagree, show both and say which is newer.
- Research goes stale. Put the date at the top, and offer to refresh it if it is over 30 days old when I next prepare for this company.
