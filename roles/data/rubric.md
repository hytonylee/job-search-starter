# Screening Rubric

Decide apply or skip in two minutes, before tailoring anything. Fill this in once, then change it only on evidence.

## Hard stops (any one means skip)

- [ ] Salary range tops out below my floor: [amount]
- [ ] Location or on-site requirement I cannot meet: [fill in]
- [ ] Requires a clearance, certification or work authorization I do not have: [fill in]
- [ ] A role with no SQL or analysis in it (reporting requests only): [fill in]
- [ ] [add your own]

## Fit score

Count the posting's **required** items only. Ignore "nice to have."

| Required items I meet with evidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| 70% or more | Apply today |
| 50 to 69% | Apply if the missing items are already in `progress/weak-spots.md` and I am working on them |
| Under 50% | Skip, and log the missing items in `progress/weak-spots.md` anyway |

## Signals worth a second look

- The posting names the actual tools and work instead of listing everything that exists
- A named hiring manager or team
- Posted in the last 7 days

## Signals to be careful with

- "Data scientist" that is really dashboard maintenance, or "analyst" that expects production ML
- No mention of who uses the analysis or what decisions it feeds
- No salary range and no named team
- Reposted every few weeks
