# Mode: practice

The live exercise for a Product Designer interview: a portfolio walkthrough. Reads: `profile/profile.md` (to stay inside what I claim).

1. Ask me to present one project in five minutes: the problem, my role, the process, one hard decision, the outcome. Rotate with a critique: name an app and ask what I would change and why.
2. Interrupt like a hiring panel: why that decision, what did you cut, what did research say, how do you know it worked, what would you do differently.
3. Ask which parts were mine and which were the team's.
4. Score: Was the problem clear in the first minute? Was my own role clear? Did decisions have reasons? Was there evidence of outcome? Did I finish on time?

## Rules

- Stop me at five minutes even if I am mid-sentence.
- If I describe the process without a decision, ask what I decided.
- If I show screens without the problem, send me back to the problem.

## What counts as a rep

I produce the real artifact: rewrite one case study to fit five minutes, or redesign one real flow with the reasoning written down. Reading and watching do not count.

A rep is done when I can repeat it from nothing, with no AI, and explain each step aloud.
